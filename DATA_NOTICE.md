# Data and third-party material

EmpiricalRelations.jl is released primarily as software under the MIT License. The
repository also contains a source-linked scientific database assembled from published
literature.

The MIT License applies to original software, database structure, extraction code, and
project documentation authored for this repository. It does not supersede copyright or
licensing terms attached to third-party publications, arXiv submissions, figures,
tables, or supplementary material.

The normalized CSV records contain bibliographic facts, numerical measurements, short
mathematical expressions, and provenance needed to identify the original source. Users
must cite the corresponding publication and determine whether their intended reuse of
source material is permitted by its publisher or author license. Publication-level
attribution and links are stored in `data/sources.csv`; record-level evidence locations
are stored in `data/provenance.csv`.

Downloaded PDFs and paper source archives are excluded from Git and from software
release archives. Their local presence does not imply redistribution permission.
